CASE STUDY / 01 · FINTECH

Payment Checkout Optimizer.

A state-aware payment checkout flow focused on clarity, recovery and transaction completion.

ROLE Product Design + UX FOCUS Payment UX · Interaction SKILLS User flows · Prototyping · Usability testing

The challenge

Payment journeys can become confusing when users do not understand payment states or what action to take after an interruption. This project focuses on clearer status communication and recovery paths.

Design focus

• Make payment status and next steps understandable.
• Map alternate and interrupted payment states.
• Design clear feedback and recovery interactions.
• Consider usability and transaction completion together.

Process

Mapped user flows, explored interaction states, created prototypes and considered usability testing as a validation step.

Next validation

Test whether users understand pending, failed and successful states. Add actual participant findings only after testing.

CASE STUDY / 02 · MERCHANT OPERATIONS

Merchant Payment Health.

A merchant dashboard designed to monitor payment performance, identify issues and support faster operational decisions.

ROLE Product + UX FOCUS Dashboard UX · Data visualization SKILLS Information architecture · Prioritization

The challenge

Merchant teams need a clear view of payment performance and issues. Scattered information can make it harder to identify what needs attention first.

Design focus

• Organize important payment information.
• Establish a clear information hierarchy.
• Use data visualization to support scanning.
• Prioritize operational issues and next actions.

Process

Focused on dashboard information architecture, metric prioritization and data visualization patterns.

Next validation

Validate whether merchants can locate important signals, understand trends and identify the next operational action.
